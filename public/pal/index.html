Ask Claude for an app. Pal makes it feel at home on your iPhone.

You can already ask an AI to make you a tool: a training log, a vocabulary drill, a recipe box. Then it ends up as a link you lose.

Pal gives it a home. Each app gets its own icon in Pal, and opens full screen on your iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Ask Siri, not the app. Siri, Shortcuts and Claude can all read from your apps and act for you, so you get answers without opening anything.

Your apps work together, because they keep what they know in one place, which only you can sign in to.
